About Pi Mu Epsilon Slide 3 of 12

About Pi Mu Epsilon

Speaker: Emma Kay

  • Founded at Syracuse University and incorporated in 1914.
  • A national honor society devoted to promoting mathematics and recognizing student achievement.
  • Chapters encourage scholarship through talks, writing, conferences, mentoring, and mathematical community.
PME encourages undergraduate presentations and posters, supports chapter activity, and sponsors a journal accessible to undergraduate students.

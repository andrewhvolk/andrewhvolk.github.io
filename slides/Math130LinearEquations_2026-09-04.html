Clear fractions before solving

Solve and check

$\dfrac{x+1}{3}-\dfrac{x-2}{4}=2$

  1. LCD $=12$. Multiply every term by $12$.
  2. $4(x+1)-3(x-2)=24$
  3. $4x+4-3x+6=24$, so $x=14$.
  4. Check: $15/3-12/4=5-3=2$.

Keep each numerator grouped until after the LCD is distributed.

Rearrange a formula with fractions

Solve for b

$d=\dfrac{a+b}{c}$

  1. Restriction: $c\ne0$.
  2. Clear the denominator: $cd=a+b$.
  3. Isolate the requested variable: $b=cd-a$.
  4. Check by substituting $cd-a$ for $b$.

Treat letters like numbers. Undo operations in reverse order and preserve grouping.

Solve a rational equation safely

Solve and verify

$\dfrac{2}{x-1}=\dfrac{3}{x+2}$

  1. Restrictions: $x\ne1,-2$.
  2. Multiply by $(x-1)(x+2)$: $2(x+2)=3(x-1)$.
  3. $2x+4=3x-3$, so $x=7$.
  4. $7$ is allowed, and both sides equal $1/3$.

Write restrictions first. A solution that makes an original denominator zero must be rejected.

From factoring to quadratic roots

Solve by factoring

$6x^2+x-2=0$

  1. $ac=-12$. Use $4$ and $-3$: $6x^2+4x-3x-2=0$.
  2. Group: $2x(3x+2)-1(3x+2)=0$.
  3. Factor: $(2x-1)(3x+2)=0$.
  4. Zero-product property: $x=\dfrac12$ or $x=-\dfrac23$.

Factoring produces expressions. Setting each factor equal to zero produces the roots.

Mixed guided practice

Work first · compare methods · then reveal
  1. Solve: $\dfrac{2x-1}{3}+\dfrac{x+2}{6}=\dfrac52$.
  2. Solve $v=\dfrac{d-d_0}{t}$ for $d$.
  3. Solve: $4x^2-12x=0$.
Solutions
  1. LCD $6$: $2(2x-1)+(x+2)=15$, so $x=3$.
  2. $vt=d-d_0$, so $d=vt+d_0$.
  3. $4x(x-3)=0$, so $x=0$ or $x=3$.

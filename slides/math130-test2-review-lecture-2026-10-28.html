Where points are usually lost

  • Using the rate as a percent (4) instead of a decimal (0.04).
  • Moving the base to the wrong side when switching log ↔ exponential form.
  • Rounding in the middle of a calculation.
  • Using degrees in s = rθ or A = ½r²θ.
  • Forgetting the seconds step in decimal → DMS.
  • Using the diameter (or the whole trapezoid side) where the formula wants a radius or height.
  • Matching the wrong segments in a proportion.
  • Leaving off units: cm², ft/min, mi/h.

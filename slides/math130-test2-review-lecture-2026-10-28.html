Where points are usually lost

  • Rate as a percent (5.2) instead of a decimal (0.052).
  • Wrong n: quarterly is 4, semiannually is 2, monthly is 12.
  • Calling a model growth because the starting value is large. Only the base decides.
  • Moving the base to the wrong side in log form.
  • Rounding in the middle of a calculation.
  • Solving for x but not finding the angle.
  • Mismatched sides in a similar-triangle proportion.
  • Skipping the semiperimeter, or a negative factor in Heron.
  • Doubling an inscribed angle instead of halving the arc.
  • Dividing seconds by 60 instead of 3600.
  • Using degrees in s = rθ or A = ½r²θ.
  • Leaving ω in rpm, or using a diameter as the radius.

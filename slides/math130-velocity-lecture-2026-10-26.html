Linear and angular velocity

Same turn, different speed

  • Both dots turn through the same angle in the same time: the same angular velocity ω.
  • The outer dot travels a bigger circle in that time, so it moves faster: a bigger linear velocity v.
  • ω = how fast it turns (radians per unit of time).
    v = how fast a point moves (distance per unit of time).
Linear and angular velocity

Predict

Ana sits 2 m from the center of a merry-go-round. Ben sits 6 m from the center. It turns once every 10 s.

Question 1Who has the larger angular velocity?

Neither. Both turn 2π rad in 10 s: ω = 2π/10 ≈ 0.628 rad/s.

Question 2Who has the larger linear velocity, and how many times larger?

Ben, 3 times larger: 6 m is 3 × 2 m. (Ana ≈ 1.26 m/s, Ben ≈ 3.77 m/s.)

Linear and angular velocity

Where v = rω comes from

v = s⁄t

On a circle, the distance traveled is an arc: s = rθ (θ in radians).

v = rθ⁄t = r · θ⁄t = rω

Linear velocity = radius × angular velocity, with ω in radians per unit of time. Radians have no units, so (ft)(rad/min) = ft/min.

Linear and angular velocity

The unit chain: five lines, every time

1
Radius. Given a diameter? Divide by 2.
2
Angular velocity in radians. rpm × 2π rad per revolution.
3
Multiply. v = rω
4
Convert units. 12 in = 1 ft · 5280 ft = 1 mi · 60 min = 1 h · 1000 m = 1 km · 3600 s = 1 h
5
Round once, at the end, and write the units.

Shortcut for rpm: v = (rpm)(2πr) gives distance per minute. It is the same as lines 2 and 3 together.

Worked example

Riverboat paddle wheel

The paddles have a radius of 8.50 ft and turn at 20.0 rpm. How fast is a paddle tip moving, in ft/min and mi/h?

1 · Radiusr = 8.50 ft
2 · Radians per minuteω = 20.0 rev⁄min · 2π rad⁄rev = 40π rad/min
3 · Multiplyv = rω = 8.50(40π) = 340π ≈ 1068.1 ft/min
4–5 · Convert, round1068.1 ft⁄min · 60 min⁄1 h · 1 mi⁄5280 ft ≈ 12.1 mi/h

Hang glider

A hang glider flies a circular arc of radius 90.0 m with angular velocity 0.125 rad/s. Find its speed in m/s and km/h.

1 · Radiusr = 90.0 m

Spot the error

Three wrong answers, one right one

A wheel 2.0 ft across turns at 300 rpm. Find the rim speed in ft/min.

Answer Av = 2.0(300)(2π) ≈ 3769.9 ft/min

Used the diameter. The answer is doubled.

Answer Bv = 1.0(300)(360) = 108,000 ft/min

Used degrees. ω must be in radians.

Answer Cv = 1.0(300) = 300 ft/min

Left ω in rpm (forgot 2π per revolution).

Answer Dv = 1.0(300)(2π) = 600π ≈ 1885.0 ft/min

Correct: radius, radians, multiply.

Wind turbine

The blades of a GE 1.5s wind turbine are 116 ft long and turn at 22.0 rpm.

a) Tip speed in ft/min and mi/h

r = 116 ft (the blade is the radius)
v = 116(22.0)(2π) = 5104π ≈ 16,035 ft/min
≈ 182.2 mi/h

b) Area swept by the blades, in acres (1 acre = 43,560 ft²)

A = πr² = π(116)² ≈ 42,273 ft²
42,273 ÷ 43,560 ≈ 0.970 acre

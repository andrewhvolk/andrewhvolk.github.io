convert angle measures and compute arc length and sector area

Formula focus
  • Radians measure arc length per radius.
  • $s=r\theta$ and $A=\tfrac12r^2\theta$ require radians.
  • Convert degrees using $\theta_{rad}=\theta_{deg}\pi/180$.

Explain · 2.5 minSource: 14, 15

Model the Skill: convert angle measures and compute arc length and sector area

1

$120^\circ=2\pi/3$ radians.

2

$s=r\theta=12(2\pi/3)=8\pi\approx25.133$ units.

3

$A=\tfrac12r^2\theta=48\pi\approx150.796$ square units.

Student practice

Find the arc length for radius 9 and central angle 40°.

Feedback and solution

$40^\circ=2\pi/9$ radians, so $s=9(2\pi/9)=2\pi$.

relate angular velocity, linear velocity, and distance

Formula focus
  • Angular speed: $\omega=\theta/t$.
  • Linear speed: $v=r\omega$.
  • Distance: $d=vt=r\theta$.

Explain · 2.5 minSource: 23

Model the Skill: relate angular velocity, linear velocity, and distance

1

$v=r\omega=4(3)=12$ ft/s.

2

$d=vt=12(10)=120$ ft.

3

Radians are dimensionless in the linear-speed conversion.

Model · 3.0 minSource: 24

You Try: relate angular velocity, linear velocity, and distance

Student practice

A wheel of radius 0.35 m turns at 8 rad/s. Find rim speed.

Feedback and solution

$v=r\omega=0.35(8)=2.8$ m/s.

Activate · 1.0 minSource: Authored

  • Require an individual prediction before discussion.

select volume and surface-area formulas for solids

1

Volume measures three-dimensional capacity; surface area measures exposed two-dimensional faces.

2

Cylinder: $V=\pi r^2h$ and $SA=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$.

Explain · 2.5 minSource: 27

Model the Skill: select volume and surface-area formulas for solids

1

$V=\pi(R^2-r^2)h$.

2

$V=\pi(4^2-3^2)(10)=70\pi$ cubic units.

3

Subtract the inner cylinder from the outer cylinder.

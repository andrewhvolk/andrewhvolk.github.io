**Class:** Friday, November 13, 2026, 50 minutes. Core instruction takes 44 minutes; preserve at least five minutes of buffer. The optional identity slide is independent review or material for a later meeting.

Students should leave able to locate a terminal side, measure its reference angle to the x-axis, explain a trigonometric sign using coordinates, and find every solution in a stated interval. Axis angles and impossible sine values provide checks on when a procedure applies.

## Responding to errors

- **Students measure to the y-axis:** return to the orange x-axis arcs. Contrast the tempting 60° angle with the 30° reference angle at 150°.
- **Students make r negative:** distinguish a signed coordinate from a distance. Trace the radial segment from the origin.
- **Students report only the calculator output:** point to both circle intersections and re-read the requested interval. Keep full calculator precision until the final rounding.
- **Students choose the same sign in every quadrant:** ask whether the relevant x or y coordinate is positive or negative before discussing a mnemonic.

If discussion runs long, reduce the number of teacher-worked degree examples. Preserve the paired radian problem, equation practice, and exit response. Defer the optional identity review and triangle-area extension.

Model the Skill: apply exponential and logarithmic models

Find the balance for $2500$ at 5.2% compounded quarterly for 6 years, then convert $\log_2(32)=5$.

  1. $A=2500(1+0.052/4)^{24}\approx3408.53$.
  2. $\log_2(32)=5$ is equivalent to $2^5=32$.
  3. The interest result uses dollars; the logarithmic conversion is dimensionless.

solve angle, arc, and sector problems

Concept
  • Convert angles before applying $s=r\theta$ or $A=\tfrac12r^2\theta$.
  • Use circle theorems only after identifying the intercepted arc.

Explain · 2.5 minSource: 5

Model the Skill: solve angle, arc, and sector problems

Convert $5\pi/6$ to degrees and find arc length for $r=9$, $\theta=2\pi/3$.

  1. $(5\pi/6)(180^\circ/\pi)=150^\circ$.
  2. $s=r\theta=9(2\pi/3)=6\pi$ units.
  3. Radians cancel in the arc-length calculation.

Explain · 2.5 minSource: 7

Model the Skill: choose area, similarity, and speed strategies

Find a trapezoid area with bases 10 and 16 and height 7, then solve $6/9=x/15$.

  1. $A=\tfrac12(10+16)(7)=91$ square units.
  2. $9x=90$, so $x=10$.
  3. Formula choice follows the given measurements and correspondence.

Feedback and solution

  • $v=r\omega=0.4(5)=2$ m/s.

select and apply triangle area and Pythagorean formulas

1

Use $A=bh/2$ when a base and perpendicular height are known.

2

Use Heron’s formula with three sides.

For an equilateral triangle, $A=s^2\sqrt3/4$.

solve similarity proportions and convert navigation angles

Concept
  • Corresponding vertices must remain in the same order.
  • DMS conversion uses 60 minutes per degree and 60 seconds per minute.
  • One nautical mile corresponds to one minute of latitude.

Explain · 2.5 minSource: 42, 43

Model the Skill: solve similarity proportions and convert navigation angles

Solve $6/9=10/x$, then convert $45^\circ22'18''$ to decimal degrees.

  1. $6x=90$, so $x=15$.
  2. $45+22/60+18/3600=45.371667^\circ$.
  3. Corresponding sides stay in the same order and DMS uses factors of 60.

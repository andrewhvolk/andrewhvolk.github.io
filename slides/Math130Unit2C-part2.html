Polygon Angle Sums and Area

1

Interior-angle sum: $(n-2)180^\circ$; convex-polygon exterior-angle sum: $360^\circ$.

2

Parallelogram area: $A=bh$. Trapezoid area: $A=\tfrac12(b_1+b_2)h$; use the perpendicular height.

3

For a regular hexagon, $e=360/6=60^\circ$ and $i=180-60=120^\circ$.

Explain · 2.0 minSource: 20, 21

Octagon, Trapezoid, and Parallelogram

Find an octagon's interior-angle sum, a trapezoid's area (bases 12 and 20, height 7), and a parallelogram's area (base 9, height 8).

1

$(8-2)180^\circ=1080^\circ$.

2

$A_{trap}=\tfrac12(12+20)(7)=112$ square units.

3

$A_{para}=bh=9(8)=72$ square units.

4

Use perpendicular height; angle measure uses degrees and area uses square units.

Triangle Area: Match the Given Information

1

Known base and height: $A=\tfrac12 bh$. For $b=10$ and $h=6$, $A=30$ square units.

2

Right triangle with a missing leg: use $a^2+b^2=c^2$, then $A=\tfrac12 ab$. Here $c$ is the hypotenuse.

3

Three sides: use Heron’s formula, $A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$, where $s=(a+b+c)/2$.

4

Equilateral triangle with side $s$: $A=s^2\sqrt3/4$.

Explain · 2.0 minSource: 31, 32

Equilateral Triangle: Exact Area

Apply the equilateral formula for side 4 units, then for side 1600 km.

1

For $s=4$, $A=4^2\sqrt3/4=4\sqrt3$ square units.

2

For $s=1600$ km, $A=1600^2\sqrt3/4=640000\sqrt3$ square kilometers.

3

$A\approx1108512.52$ square kilometers.

4

Keep the exact radical form unless an approximation is requested.

Heron’s Formula: A Worked Example

Find the area of a triangle with sides 5, 6, and 7 units. Give exact form, then round to two decimal places.

1

Check $5+6>7$. The semiperimeter is $s=(5+6+7)/2=9$.

2

Then $s-a=4$, $s-b=3$, and $s-c=2$.

3

$A=\sqrt{9(4)(3)(2)}=\sqrt{216}=6\sqrt6$ square units.

4

$A\approx14.70$ square units. Round after finding the exact value.

  • Emphasize the semiperimeter and show all three subtractions.

Right Triangle: Height Before Area

A right triangle has base 8 and hypotenuse 17 units. Find its perpendicular height, then its area.

1

The 17-unit side is opposite the right angle, so it is the hypotenuse.

2

$h^2+8^2=17^2$, so $h^2=289-64=225$.

3

$h=\sqrt{225}=15$ units. A length is positive.

4

$A=\tfrac12(8)(15)=60$ square units.

Model · 1.5 minSource: Authored

  • Ask why the hypotenuse is not the height.

Similarity and Navigation Angles

Concept
  • Corresponding vertices must remain in the same order.
  • DMS conversion uses 60 minutes per degree and 60 seconds per minute.
  • One minute of latitude is approximately one nautical mile. One nautical mile is exactly 1852 m.
  • Example: $5$ nmi $=5(1852)=9260$ m $=9.26$ km.

Explain · 2.0 minSource: 42, 43

  • The Earth detail is schematic and magnified. Angular minutes are not minutes of time.
  • Nautical-mile definition: IALA Dictionary, https://www.iala.int/wiki/dictionary/index.php/Nautical_mile. Angular subdivisions: NOAA, https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/latitude.html.

Similarity Proportions and DMS

Match the sides to solve $6/9=10/x$. Convert $45^\circ22'18''$ to decimal degrees, rounded to six decimal places.

  1. $6x=90$, so $x=15$.
  2. Add $45+22/60+18/3600$ to obtain approximately $45.371667^\circ$.
  3. Corresponding sides stay in the same order and DMS uses factors of 60.

Similar Triangles: Angles and Shrinking

For $\triangle ABC\sim\triangle DEF$, find $\angle D$, $\angle F$, $DF$, and $EF$. Round angles to 0.1° and lengths to 0.01 unit.

  1. $\angle D\approx59.5^\circ$. The third angle is $180-59.5-53.1=67.4^\circ$, so $\angle F\approx67.4^\circ$.
  2. The scale from ABC to DEF is $k=DE/AB=10/15=2/3<1$.
  3. $DF=13(2/3)=26/3\approx8.67$ units.
  4. $EF=14(2/3)=28/3\approx9.33$ units. Apply the same factor to both sides.

Model · 2.5 minSource: Authored

  • Corresponding vertices are A-D, B-E, C-F.
  • The given angle labels are rounded to a tenth. Check the rounded third angle against the actual triangle geometry.

Decimal Degrees to DMS: Successive Remainders

Convert $135.721^\circ$ to DMS. Keep seconds to one decimal place.

1

The whole degrees are $135$. The fractional degree part is $0.721$.

2

$0.721(60)=43.26$: keep $43$ minutes and the remainder $0.26$.

3

$0.26(60)=15.6$ seconds.

4

$135.721^\circ=135^\circ43'15.6''$.

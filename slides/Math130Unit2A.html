Review: Exponent Rules

Before we begin, ensure you are comfortable with these properties:

  • Product Rule: $\quad a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}$
  • Power Rule: $\quad (a^m)^n = a^{mn}$
  • Quotient Rule: $\quad \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$
  • Zero Exponent: $\quad a^0 = 1 \quad (a \neq 0)$
  • Negative Exponent: $\quad a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$
  • Fractional: $\quad a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a}$

Defining Exponential Functions

The Formal Definition

Let $b$ be a constant with $b > 0$ and $b \neq 1$.

Then for any real number $x$, the function $f(x) = b^x$ is an exponential function of base $b$.

Universal Properties:

  • Domain: $(-\infty, \infty)$
  • Range: $(0, \infty)$
  • y-intercept: $(0, 1)$
  • Asymptote: $y = 0$
Base ($b$): 2.0

Simple vs. Compound Interest

Simple Interest ($I = Prt$): Interest is only earned on the original principal.

Compound Interest: Interest is earned on the principal AND previously earned interest.

Scenario: Jenny invests $70,000 at 3% compounded annually. Frank invests $70,000 at 3% simple interest. Compare the first 3 years.

Year Frank (Simple) Jenny (Compound)
1st $2,100.00 $2,100.00
2nd $2,100.00 $2,163.00
3rd $2,100.00 $2,227.89

The Takeaway: The gap grows every year because Jenny earns "interest on her interest".

Modeling: Radioactive Decay

Uranium-240 has a half-life of 14 hours. The amount remaining (in grams) after $t$ hours is modeled by:

$$A(t) = 3200 \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/14} \text{ }$$

Step 1: Find the initial amount.

Plug in $t = 0$:

$A(0) = 3200(1/2)^0$
$A(0) = 3200(1) = \mathbf{3,200 \text{ g}}$

Step 2: Find the amount after 40 hours.

Plug in $t = 40$:

$A(40) = 3200(1/2)^{40/14}$
$A(40) \approx \mathbf{442 \text{ g}}$

Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Base vs. Exponent

Confusing $x^2$ with $2^x$.

$x^2$ is a power function (variable base). $2^x$ is exponential. They grow at completely different rates.

2. Decimal Conversions

Using $r = 5$ instead of $r = 0.05$.

Always divide your percentage by 100! If your answer seems absurdly large, check this first.

3. The Wrong 'n' Value

Confusing $n$ and $t$.

$n$ is compounds per year (Quarterly = 4). It is NOT the number of years.

4. Wrong Formula

Using $A = P(1+\frac{r}{n})^{nt}$ for continuous.

If the problem says "compounded continuously," use $A = Pe^{rt}$. There is no $n$!

5. Forgetting Calculator Parentheses

Typing $(3/4)^{-2}$ as $3/4^{-2}$ in your calculator.

Always parenthesize the base and the exponent. When in doubt, add more parentheses.

Synthesis & Key Takeaways

  • Structure: $f(x) = b^x$ is exponential because the variable is the exponent.
  • Behavior: $b > 1$ represents growth, while $0 < b < 1$ represents decay.
  • The Natural Base: $e \approx 2.71828$ is used primarily for continuous compounding: $A = Pe^{rt}$.
  • Periodic Finance: Use $A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$, ensuring $r$ is a decimal and $n$ is compounds per year.

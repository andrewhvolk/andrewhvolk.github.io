1 Component form from points

Rule: vector = terminal point − initial point

v = ⟨x₂ − x₁, y₂ − y₁⟩

Multiply every component first.

½w + u = ½⟨10,14⟩ + ⟨−3,4⟩
= ⟨, ⟩ + ⟨−3,4⟩

Skyhawk Review of Math 130 7.1 Skills

Welcome to your checkride out of Lynchburg (LYH). You will review and practice the 4 key right-triangle trigonometry skills required for your flight path:
  • ☑ Stage 1: Elevation (Ascent distance & time)
  • ☑ Stage 2: Bearing (North/South & East/West components)
  • ☑ Stage 3: Depression (Descent altitude loss)
  • ☑ Stage 4: Return (Distance & Bearing to origin)
Important: Always round to the designated precision. If you miss a calculation, ATC will show you the math and give you a new heading to try.

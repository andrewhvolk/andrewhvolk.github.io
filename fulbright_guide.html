Fulbright Pathway

A definitive guide for Math, Applied Math, and Physics undergraduates at Liberty University preparing for the Fulbright U.S. Student Program.

Liberty University Fulbright Advisor

All prospective applicants must work with Professor Edna Udobong, Liberty's Fulbright Program Advisor. You must contact her at internationalscholarships@liberty.edu to begin the process.

Requirement: Liberty mandates completion of a "Canvas Fulbright Application Summer Training" course prior to applying.

The Undergraduate Rule

Can I go on a Fulbright while I am still an undergrad? No. You must have your bachelor's degree before the start of the grant. However, you must apply during your senior year (or as an alumni). Whether you graduate in December or May, you apply in the Fall of your final academic year.

STEM Focus: Study/Research Grants Requirements: Host Affiliation & Proposal

Inspiration: Where Can STEM Majors Go?

Hungary: BSM Math Program

Hungary offers a highly prestigious, discipline-specific award: the Fulbright / Budapest Semesters in Mathematics (BSM) at the Rényi Institute. It is tailor-made for high-level math students to take advanced coursework and join elite research groups in Central Europe.

Applied Physics in Europe

Past Physics undergrads have won grants to pursue an MSc. in Applied Physics at Delft University of Technology (Netherlands) researching solar cell nanocrystals, or joined labs like Thales Research and Technology (France) for optical physics.

Find Your Liberty Mentor

Liberty Tip: Seek out faculty like Dr. Esswein. As a former Fulbright scholar to Eastern Europe himself, he has successfully mentored multiple Liberty students to win their own Fulbright Awards and P.A.R.E. research grants!

The Anatomy of a STEM Application

Statement of Grant Purpose

2 pages. The core of your application. What research will you do, and why must it be done in that specific country?

Personal Statement

1 page. Your narrative. How did your background in Math/Physics lead you to this exact moment and proposal?

Letter of Affiliation

A signed letter from your host university/lab abroad explicitly inviting you to conduct your proposed research.

3 Recommendation Letters

Usually from Liberty faculty. For STEM, these should speak heavily to your technical competence and research ability.

Why apply? What does it cover?

The Fulbright is fully funded by the U.S. Department of State. If you win, your focus is entirely on your research and cultural exchange.

The Active Applicant Track

You are applying for the 2027-2028 Grant Year. Your application is due THIS Fall (2026).

Summer/Fall 2026: Liberty Deadlines

  • August 5 - Canvas Training Due

    Deadline to complete Liberty's mandatory Canvas Fulbright Application Summer Training.

  • August 15 - Liberty Campus Deadline

    Strict Campus Deadline: Complete online application must be submitted via the portal to be eligible for the campus interview.

  • Early September - Campus Interview

    Attend the mandatory campus interview with Liberty University's Fulbright Committee. Feedback will be provided to polish your STEM essays.

  • 2nd Tuesday in October - National Deadline

    Final submission to the national Fulbright portal. No changes can be made after this date.

Spring 2027: The Waiting Period

  • Late January - Semi-Finalist Notification

    The U.S. National Screening Committee announces if your application has been forwarded to the host country for final review.

  • March to May - Finalist Notification

    Host countries notify applicants on a rolling basis. If selected, you begin pre-departure orientations and visa paperwork.

  • May 2027 (For May Grads)

    Graduate from Liberty University! Your grant will typically start between August 2027 and March 2028 depending on the country.

December 2026 Grads Note:

If you graduate in Dec '26, you will have a "gap semester" (Spring 2027) before your grant begins. Use this time to work, do research, or study the language of your host country.

The Preparation Track

You will apply in Fall 2027 for the 2028-2029 Grant Year. Now is the time to build your profile and secure funding.

Fall 2026: Profile & P.A.R.E.

  • Email Prof. Udobong

    Send an introductory email to internationalscholarships@liberty.edu to get on the radar for the 28-29 cycle.

  • Apply for the P.A.R.E. Grant

    Liberty offers the Provost's Award for Research Excellence (up to $2,500) specifically for students promising to apply for a Fulbright. Apply in late Fall/Winter for summer research funding.

  • Identify STEM Interests

    Work with Liberty Math/Physics faculty to define a research niche (e.g., condensed matter, topology) that you want to pursue abroad.

Spring 2027: Boot Camp & Affiliation

  • March/April - Liberty Boot Camp

    Attend Liberty's official Fulbright information sessions and the Spring Application Boot Camp. Register for the Summer Canvas Training.

  • The Affiliation Hunt

    CRITICAL STEP: Start cold-emailing professors and labs in your target country. You must secure a host willing to oversee your research project.

  • May - Execute P.A.R.E. Research

    If awarded the P.A.R.E., spend your summer conducting your funded research while simultaneously drafting your Fulbright essays for the August 15 deadline.

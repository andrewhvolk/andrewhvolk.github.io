Fulbright Pathway: Math & Physics

A definitive guide for Math, Applied Math, and Physics undergraduates at Liberty University preparing for the Fulbright U.S. Student Program.

The Undergraduate Rule

Can I go on a Fulbright while I am still an undergrad? No. You must have your bachelor's degree before the start of the grant. However, you must apply during your senior year (or as an alumni). Whether you graduate in December or May, you apply in the Fall of your final academic year.

Anatomy of a STEM Application

  • Statement of Grant Purpose (2 pages): The technical details of your proposed math/physics research.
  • Personal Statement (1 page): Your narrative. Why math? Why this country?
  • Letter of Affiliation: A signed letter from a foreign professor agreeing to host you.
  • 3 Letters of Recommendation: Usually from LU Math/Physics faculty.

What Does the Grant Cover?

The Fulbright program is fully funded by the U.S. Department of State. If selected, you receive:

  • Round-trip airfare to your host country
  • Monthly living stipend (covers rent & food)
  • Basic health benefits
  • In some countries: Full tuition coverage

Which Grant is Right for You?

Study/Research Grant

Design your own independent math or physics research project working alongside foreign scientists. Best for students aiming for a PhD.

English Teaching Assistant (ETA) Grant

Help teach English in foreign schools. Secret Advantage: Many countries specifically request STEM majors to help teach math/science in English!

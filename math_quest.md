# Math Quest: A Static Browser Game Concept

## 1. Core Concept

Math Quest is a 2D static browser game where the player solves math problems to defeat enemies and progress through different levels. The game focuses on basic arithmetic, algebra, and geometry concepts. The player takes on the role of a mathematics student.

## 2. Player Character

*   **The Mathematician:** A character (male or female) with basic movement and the ability to solve math problems.
*   **Abilities:** Solving equations, using geometric formulas, and applying mathematical principles.

## 3. Gameplay Mechanics

*   **Course Encounter:** The player contacts an enemy representing a course.
*   **Multiple-Choice Challenge:** The player is presented with a multiple-choice question related to the course.
*   **Grading System:**
    *   First try: A
    *   Second try: B
    *   Third try: C
    *   Failure: Restart the level
*   **Level Progression:** The game is structured into 8 semesters (levels). Each semester focuses on different math courses. Each level spawns a canvas where the character can jump, navigate, and encounter enemies.
*   **Power-ups/Collectibles:**
    *   **Formulas:** Provide hints or solutions to specific types of problems.
    *   **Tools:** Help with calculations or problem-solving (e.g., calculator, protractor).
    *   **Points:** Used to unlock new levels or character customizations.
*   **Boss Battles:** Represent challenging math concepts or major exams.

## 4. Enemy Design (Course-Based)

Enemies are themed visually and mechanically around the math course they represent. Each level (semester) contains 4-7 enemies.

*   **Example Enemies:**
    *   **Semester 1:**
        *   `MATH 101 (Calculus I)`: Derivative Demon, Integral Imp, Limit Lurker
        *   `ENGL 101 (Freshman Composition)`: Grammar Goblin, Syntax Serpent, Thesis Terror
    *   **Semester 2:**
        *   `MATH 102 (Calculus II)`: Series Specter, Sequence Shade, Convergence Creature
        *   `PHYS 101 (Physics I)`: Projectile Phantom, Force Fiend, Energy Entity

## 5. World & Level Design

*   **Level Themes:** Each semester takes place in a different area of the university. The levels are designed as canvases where the player can move around.
    *   **Semester 1:** Lecture Hall
    *   **Semester 2:** Library

## 6. Potential Features

*   **Difficulty Levels:** Adjust the complexity of the math problems.
*   **Timed Challenges:** Solve problems quickly to earn bonus points.

## 7. Art Style and Visual Presentation

*   **Visual Style:** Simple 2D graphics suitable for a static browser game.
*   **Character Design:** Cartoonish and expressive characters.
*   **UI Design:** Clear and intuitive interface for displaying problems and solutions.

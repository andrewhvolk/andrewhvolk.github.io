Section 5.1–5.2 Foundations

Memorize
Arc Length
$$s=r\theta$$ $$s=\frac{\theta}{360}(2\pi r)$$
Use radian form when $\\theta$ is in radians, degree form when in degrees.
Memorize
Right-Triangle Ratios
$$\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}}$$
$$\cos\theta=\frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}$$
$$\tan\theta=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}$$
Core relationships for side/angle solving in right triangles.
Useful
Coterminal Angles
$$\theta_{\text{coterminal}} = \theta + 360^\circ k \quad \text{or} \quad \theta + 2\pi k$$
Use integer $k$ to generate coterminal angles in degrees or radians.

Section 5.3 & 7.1 Applications

Memorize
Reference Angle by Quadrant
$$QII:\ \alpha=180^\circ-\theta$$
$$QIII:\ \alpha=\theta-180^\circ$$
$$QIV:\ \alpha=360^\circ-\theta$$
Use degree equivalents with $\pi$ for radian forms.
Memorize
Distance-Rate-Time
$$d=rt$$
Used with trig setup in many Section 7.1 word problems.
Memorize
Solve Right Triangle via Inverse Trig
$$\theta=\sin^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}}\right)$$
$$\theta=\cos^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}\right)$$
$$\theta=\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}\right)$$
Pick the inverse function matching known sides.

Section 8.4 Vectors

Memorize
Component Form from Points
$$\vec v=\langle x_2-x_1,\ y_2-y_1\rangle$$
From initial point $(x_1,y_1)$ to terminal point $(x_2,y_2)$.
Memorize
Vector Magnitude
$$|\vec v|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\quad \text{for }\vec v=\langle a,b\rangle$$
Length of a vector in component form.
Memorize
Direction Angle
$$\theta=\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{b}{a}\right)$$
Adjust for quadrant when determining the final direction angle.
Useful
Vector from Magnitude & Direction
$$\vec v=\langle |\vec v|\cos\theta,\ |\vec v|\sin\theta\rangle$$
Useful when converting directional information to components.

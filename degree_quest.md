# Degree Quest: A College Adventure Game Concept

## 1. Core Concept

Degree Quest is a 2D side-scrolling adventure/platformer game where the player takes on the role of a university student navigating their chosen degree program. The core gameplay loop involves battling and defeating enemies that represent the actual courses required to graduate. Successfully defeating a course "enemy" signifies completing that course, allowing the player to progress through their academic journey.

## 2. Player Character

*   **The Student:** A customizable avatar representing the player. Customization could include appearance, starting "major," etc.
*   **Abilities:** Basic movement (run, jump), and combat abilities themed around learning and studying (e.g., "Focused Strike," "Memorization Shield," "Problem-Solving Blast").

## 3. Gameplay Mechanics

*   **Side-Scrolling Platforming:** Navigate diverse levels themed around university environments (campus grounds, library, lecture halls, labs).
*   **Combat:** Engage enemies representing courses using the student's abilities. Defeating an enemy clears the "course."
*   **Progression:** Levels are structured based on semesters and prerequisite chains. Completing introductory courses unlocks more advanced ones.
*   **Power-ups/Collectibles:**
    *   **Study Aids:** Temporary boosts (e.g., increased attack power, defense).
    *   **Textbooks/Notes:** Permanent upgrades or unlock new skills/information.
    *   **GPA Points:** Score/currency used for upgrades?
*   **Boss Battles:** Represent major exams (midterms, finals) or particularly challenging "weed-out" courses. These require mastering specific mechanics learned in previous course-enemies.

## 4. Enemy Design (Course-Based)

Enemies are themed visually and mechanically around the subject matter of the course they represent.

*   **Example Major: Mechanical Engineering**
    *   **Early Game (Gen Eds):**
        *   `English 101`: Enemies throwing books, using "red pen" slashing attacks, creating confusing sentence structures as obstacles.
        *   `Calculus 1`: Enemies using derivative "whips," launching limit projectiles, creating integral barriers.
    *   **Mid Game (Core Physics/Math):**
        *   `PHYS 201 (Mechanics)`: Enemies manipulating gravity, using projectile motion attacks, creating force-field obstacles. Boss could be a large "Newton's Cradle" or a complex Rube Goldberg machine.
        *   `MATH 251 (Differential Equations)`: Enemies that split into smaller parts (differentiation), or combine (integration), phase in and out based on functions.
    *   **Late Game (Advanced ME):**
        *   `Thermodynamics`: Enemies using heat/cold attacks, pressure waves, phase changes (solid/liquid/gas forms).
        *   `Fluid Mechanics`: Enemies creating currents, whirlpools, shooting high-pressure jets.
        *   `Materials Science`: Enemies with high defense, different material properties (brittle, ductile), stress/strain attacks.
*   **General Enemy Ideas:**
    *   `Chemistry`: Potion-throwing, elemental attacks (fire, acid), creating hazardous chemical spills.
    *   `Biology`: Enemies that multiply, evolve, or use organic-themed attacks.
    *   `Computer Science`: Enemies creating logic gates, firing "syntax errors," glitching/teleporting.
    *   `History`: Enemies summoning historical figures/events as attacks.

## 5. World & Level Design

*   **Hub World:** The main university campus, allowing access to different "colleges" or "departments" (level select areas).
*   **Level Themes:**
    *   **Lecture Halls:** Classic platforming, dodging projectiles from a "lecturer" enemy.
    *   **Libraries:** Maze-like, stealth sections, enemies based on finding information.
    *   **Laboratories:** Hazard-filled environments (chemical spills, electrical arcs), puzzle elements based on experiments.
    *   **Study Lounges:** Areas for upgrades, saving progress, interacting with NPCs.

## 6. Potential Features

*   **Multiple Majors:** Different paths through the game with unique course enemies and storylines.
*   **Experience Points (XP) & Leveling:**
    *   Earn XP by defeating course-enemies, completing mini-games (labs, quizzes), finding hidden "Extra Credit" collectibles, or fulfilling NPC quests.
    *   Accumulating enough XP results in a "Level Up," signifying academic growth.
    *   Leveling up grants Skill Points to invest in the Skill Tree and potentially increases base stats (Health, "Focus Meter" for special abilities, base Attack Damage).
*   **Skill Tree & Combat Stats:**
    *   **Structure:** The Skill Tree could branch into categories like:
        *   `Core Study Habits`: Skills improving general performance (e.g., faster attack speed "Quick Thinking", increased defense "Resilience", better resource management "Efficiency", improved loot drops "Resourcefulness").
        *   `Combat Techniques`: Unlocking new attack types or enhancing existing ones (e.g., area-of-effect "Broad Understanding", charged attack "Deep Dive", defensive parry "Critical Rebuttal").
        *   `Major Specialization`: Skills unique to the chosen major, providing powerful bonuses or abilities relevant to that field (e.g., `Circuit Analysis` for Electrical Engineering granting chain lightning attacks, `Organic Synthesis` for Chemistry creating powerful AoE effects, `Proof by Induction` for Math creating temporary clones).
    *   **Skill Points:** Gained upon leveling up, used to unlock or upgrade skills in the tree.
    *   **Attack Damage:** Base damage increases slightly with level. Skills can provide percentage boosts, add elemental/conceptual damage types (e.g., "Logic Damage," "Creative Damage," "Mathematical Damage"), or add status effects (e.g., "Confusion," "Burnout"). Specific gear ("Textbooks," "Lab Equipment") could also modify damage.
*   **Mini-Games:** Represent labs, quizzes, or group projects. Success could grant significant XP or temporary buffs.
*   **NPCs:** Professors, TAs, fellow students offering quests (side-assignments), tips, or challenges. Could act as vendors for study aids or gear.
*   **Story:** A lighthearted narrative about the challenges and triumphs of university life.

## 7. Narrative and Story

*   **Overall Tone:** Humorous and relatable, capturing the essence of college life.
*   **Story Arcs:**
    *   **Main Story:** The player's journey through their chosen degree program, facing academic challenges and personal growth.
    *   **Side Quests:** Optional storylines involving NPCs, exploring campus lore, or tackling extracurricular activities.
*   **Character Development:** The player character evolves throughout the game, gaining knowledge, skills, and relationships.
*   **Ending:** Multiple endings based on the player's choices and academic performance.

## 8. Art Style and Visual Presentation

*   **Visual Style:** 2D side-scrolling with a cartoonish and vibrant art style.
*   **Character Design:** Expressive and customizable characters with unique animations.
*   **Environment Design:** Detailed and immersive environments that capture the atmosphere of a university campus.
*   **UI Design:** Clean and intuitive user interface that provides clear information to the player.

## 9. Sound Design and Music

*   **Music:** Upbeat and energetic soundtrack that complements the gameplay and enhances the overall experience.
*   **Sound Effects:** Immersive sound effects that bring the game world to life.
*   **Voice Acting:** Optional voice acting for key characters and story moments.

## 10. Detailed Level Design

*   **Lecture Hall Example:** A level with rows of desks as platforms, projector beams as moving obstacles, and paper airplanes as enemy projectiles.
*   **Library Example:** A maze-like level with bookshelves as walls, students studying as stationary enemies, and librarians as patrolling enemies.

## 11. Specific Enemy Stats and Abilities

*   **English 101 Enemy:**
    *   Health: 50
    *   Attack: Throws books for 10 damage.
    *   Ability: Creates confusing sentence structures as temporary obstacles.
*   **Calculus 1 Enemy:**
    *   Health: 75
    *   Attack: Uses derivative "whips" for 15 damage.
    *   Ability: Launches limit projectiles that deal 20 damage.

## 12. Detailed Skill Tree

*   **Core Study Habits:**
    *   Quick Thinking: Increases attack speed by 10%.
    *   Resilience: Increases defense by 15%.
*   **Combat Techniques:**
    *   Broad Understanding: Area-of-effect attack that deals 25 damage to all nearby enemies.
    *   Deep Dive: Charged attack that deals 50 damage to a single enemy.

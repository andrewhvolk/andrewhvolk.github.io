# Week 5: Sound & Resonance Lab - Two-Part Experiment Instructions
Greetings Physics Students!  

As you are now finishing up the assignment and quiz requirements for Week 4 on Newton's Second Law, we can begin looking at what comes before us in the next week.

You will need to navigate to Week 5 – Sound and Resonance" where you will find the PDF document for the laboratory exercise within the Learn section.

This week you will use a tuning fork to generate standing waves in a tube which is open at one end. Through this experiment, you will identify the length of tube necessary for the sound of the tuning fork to be amplified through resonance. You will then calculate the speed of sound and the wavelength of sound generated by the tuning fork.

This week's lab involves two parts.  The first part you will be calculating the theoretical speed of sound based upon the measured temperature.  The second part of the lab does NOT use the results from the first.  You will measure lengths (L1, L2, L3) when you hear increased volume from the tuning forks.  These lengths will then be used by the corresponding equations to determine the wavelength for each case.  This wavelength is then multiplied by the frequency of the tuning fork in each case to determine three values of the speed of sound.  These can then be compared with your initial calculation.

**Important:** Record your measured tube lengths (L1, L2, L3) in the 'Measured Length (L)' column of the provided data table. Do NOT try to calculate what these lengths should be - measure the actual lengths where you hear maximum resonance, as some experimental error is expected and normal.

The activities for this week include:  

Work through the Sound and Resonance laboratory manual segments using the Sound and Resonance Instructions as a guide and template.
After completing the report worksheets within the report instructions, submit your report in Canvas.

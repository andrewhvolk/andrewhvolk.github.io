3 · The journal ladder

Four peer-reviewed homes where an engaging math-meets-floristry review is the expected genre.

Plan A

Journal of Humanistic Mathematics

Open access, zero fees, double-anonymous review; publishes aesthetic and pedagogical mathematics for a general mathematical audience. Slow but kind.

Plan B

Journal of Mathematics and the Arts

The strongest topical fit in existence for this paper; indexed Taylor & Francis title; expository math–art reviews are core scope.

Plan C

Bridges 2027

Peer-reviewed proceedings and a joyful math-art community; condensed version; attendance required — and they would adore a live spiral hand-tie.

Plan D

HortTechnology

Floral-design education readership already invested in arrangement research; verify ASHS fees first.

4 · Export

The .3mf stores every surface's Desmos color as per‑face color on one assembled object. Drag it onto an empty plate in Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer and accept the Standard 3MF Color Parsing prompt: the slicer reads the seven colours and matches or appends filaments to them. It also opens with colour in the Windows 3D Viewer and other 3MF tools. On a single‑extruder printer you can still print it by mapping those colours to an AMS or pausing to swap filament.

The .scad holds one polyhedron per surface inside a union(); its coloured preview matches Desmos, but note that pressing F6 and exporting STL drops colour, since STL stores no colour and the union fuses the parts. The direct .stl is likewise geometry only. Use the .3mf when colour matters.

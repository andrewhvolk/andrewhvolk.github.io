🧠 Math Club Leadership Team

Structure, Roles, and Members for 2025-2026

Faculty Advisor: Mr. Andrew Volk

🎯 Our Mission

The Math Club Leadership Team is dedicated to fostering a vibrant mathematical community at Liberty University. Our mission is to plan and execute engaging events, provide academic and professional resources for members, and create an inclusive environment where students from all backgrounds can explore their passion for mathematics.

👥 Officer Roles & Responsibilities

🗣️ President

Acts as the primary spokesperson and provides oversight for all club activities. Responsible for liaising with faculty and members, organizing meetings, and guiding the club's overall direction.

🎯 Vice President

Assists the President with facilitating club activities and provides oversight in their absence. Aids in the planning and preparation of club events and supports other officers.

📋 Secretary

Responsible for all club records, including meeting minutes and attendance. Assists with projects and ensures that information is organized and distributed effectively to members.

💰 Treasurer

Manages the club's finances, including receiving and handling money and monitoring the budget. Keeps a record of all transactions and ensures financial guidelines are met.

🔬 Research Team Lead

Responsible for leading team meetings, allocating work, and reviewing/revising any papers, presentations, or posters. Will be the main point of contact for the research supervising faculty member.

📊 Research Project Manager

Responsible for scheduling and planning meetings, documenting research progress and meeting notes, and setting timelines and deadlines for project.

📈 Treasury Analyst

Responsible for coordinating with Treasurer to manage the club's finances, budget appropriately, and analyze spending trends. In charge of preparing financial summaries for club meetings using spreadsheet from Treasurer.

📱 Social Media Manager

Responsible for weekly communication on GroupMe platform and/or Discord as well as regular posting on Instagram and X from club account.

🎨 Creative Manager

Assists the Social Media Manager with posts and communication through the creation of media, posts, and flyers using online platforms such as Canva.

🧠 Leadership Advisory Council

Consists of active members who contribute ideas and support planning without holding formal officer roles. Council members are an extension of the leadership team and may lead projects, subcommittees, or represent student feedback.

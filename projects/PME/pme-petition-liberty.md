## IV. Learning Resources

**A. Library:**

The Jerry Falwell Library provides access to a wide variety of print and electronic resources, including over 400,000 books, movies, scores and more in the physical collection, plus access to over 200,000 journals, magazines, newspapers and other periodicals. The library provides research assistance for students having trouble finding materials.

1. Student access to books and journals:
   
   a. Electronic resources:
   - MathSciNet: Reviews, abstracts, and bibliographic information for mathematical sciences literature, including bibliographic data for articles dating back to the early 1800s
   - IOPscience: Full-text peer-reviewed articles on energy, engineering, physics, materials science, environmental sciences, mathematics, and more
   - ScienceDirect: Full-text peer-reviewed articles on a variety of scientific topics including medicine, engineering, chemistry, computer science, and more
   - Scopus: Peer-reviewed article abstracts for a variety of scientific fields such as engineering, technology, health, medicine, and more (updated daily)
   - Solid State and Superconductivity Abstracts (Advanced Technologies & Aerospace Index): Peer-reviewed article abstracts on aeronautics, applied physics, artificial intelligence, computer programming, software engineering, and more
   - Taylor & Francis Online: Full-text, peer-reviewed articles on behavioral sciences, computer science, education, humanities, literature, medicine, social sciences, and more
   - Additional databases available through the Mathematics research guide: https://libguides.liberty.edu/c.php?g=564101&p=3884367

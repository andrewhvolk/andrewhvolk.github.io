👥Current Leadership Structure

🗣️ President

Acts as the primary spokesperson for the club and provides oversight for all official club activities. Responsible for acting as a liaison among club members, organizing club activities, and overseeing the schedule.

🎯 Vice President

Assists with the facilitation of club activities and provides oversight in absence of the President. Aids in the planning and preparation of club events.

📋 Secretary

Responsible for keeping club records, including club attendance. Assists with projects as needed and ensures that proper information is organized and distributed.

💰 Treasurer

Receives and handles club money and monitors the budget. Keeps a record of all transactions and ensures that established guidelines are met.

🧠 Leadership Advisory Council

Consists of active members who contribute ideas and support planning without necessarily holding formal officer roles. Council members serve as an extension of the leadership team and may take on projects, subcommittees, or represent student feedback.

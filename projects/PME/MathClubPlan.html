📅 Recurring Event Series

We host a series of recurring events every Monday from 10:20-11:45 AM. Join us for any and all of them!

First Mondays:
Math Challenge

  • Tackle challenging problems from competitions like the Putnam. A great way to sharpen your problem-solving skills.

Second Mondays:
Learn & Play

  • Hands-on workshops for software like LaTeX, Sage, Manim, and Jupyter. Learn tools of the trade in a fun environment.

Third Mondays:
GRE Study Group

  • Peer-led study sessions focused on the quantitative section of the GRE. Work together to prepare for grad school applications.

Fourth Mondays:
Career Prep

  • Get ready for the future with résumé reviews, interview coaching, and advice on applying for jobs and graduate programs.

🎉 Special Events & Competitions

Beyond our weekly series, we host the Estimathon, Integration Bee, conference trips, and more!

🏛️ Math Honor Society (PME & KME)

The Math Club facilitates activities for the university's mathematics honor societies. For decades, Liberty has hosted the Virginia Iota chapter of Kappa Mu Epsilon (KME), which was chartered in 1989.

Starting in Fall 2025, we are excited to launch a new chapter of Pi Mu Epsilon (PME), the National Mathematics Honor Society. This will expand opportunities for our students to gain recognition for their mathematical achievements.

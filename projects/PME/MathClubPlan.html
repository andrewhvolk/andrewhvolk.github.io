🏛️ PME Chapter Notes

Pi Mu Epsilon Integration

  • Application to PME national office is in progress
  • Aim to hold first PME induction in Fall 2025
  • All PME events coordinated by Math Club; no parallel governance structure
  • Special considerations being discussed regarding online student eligibility

PME Structure Alignment

  • PME President = Highest-ranking PME-inducted Math Club officer
  • All PME events and responsibilities coordinated through Math Club
  • No separate PME leadership team; positions mirror Math Club roles

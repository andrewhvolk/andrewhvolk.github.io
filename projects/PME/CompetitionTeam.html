🏆 Math Club Competition Team Plan

Structure, Goals, and Timeline for 2025-2026

Faculty Advisor: Mr. Andrew Volk

🎯 Mission & Core Goals

The Competition Team track is for students who enjoy collaborative problem-solving and want to represent Liberty University at regional and national mathematics competitions. Participation sharpens analytical skills, builds teamwork, and provides a fun, challenging outlet for mathematical talent.

Our primary goals for the 2025-2026 academic year are:
  • To field a competitive team for at least one major competition each semester.
  • To host regular training sessions and workshops focused on problem-solving strategies.
  • To increase student participation and confidence in tackling challenging math problems.
  • To host an engaging internal competition (the Integration Bee) during the spring semester.

🏗️ Team Structure & Process

Practice Sessions

The team will meet regularly for practice sessions. These sessions will involve working through problems from past competitions, discussing different approaches, and learning advanced problem-solving techniques.

How to Join

The Competition Team is open to all undergraduate students, regardless of major. An interest meeting will be held at the beginning of the Fall semester. Come ready to solve some fun problems!

🗓️ Proposed Semester Timeline

This schedule is designed to prepare our team for key competitions and build a strong problem-solving culture within the club.

Fall 2025 Semester

Primary Target: William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition in December.
  • September 2025
    Team Kickoff & Putnam Prep Begins

    Host an initial interest meeting during a Math Club convo slot or evening, depending on availability. Prior to the meeting, assign two Putnam problems (e.g., A1 and A2) for students to attempt. During the meeting, discuss the format of the competition (two 3-hour sessions, no partial credit, focus on number theory/combinatorics/probability), and have students present solutions on a whiteboard.

  • October 2025
    Intensive Training & Mock Exam

    Deepen the training on specific proof techniques and problem areas. Hold a full-length mock Putnam exam to simulate test conditions.

  • November 2025
    Advanced Strategy & Problem Workshops

    Focus on analyzing past Putnam problems, discussing high-level strategies, and refining proof-writing skills.

  • December 2025
    Final Review & Putnam Competition

    Participate in the Putnam exam on the first Saturday of December.

Strategy & Collaboration Tools

  • Maintain a shared Google Doc with key theorems and problem-solving strategies.
  • Create a dedicated Putnam practice channel in the Math Club Discord.
  • Encourage team members to solve and discuss additional A-level problems collaboratively each week.

Spring 2026 Semester

Primary Target: Host the Liberty University Integration Bee.
  • January & February 2026
    Problem-Solving Workshops

    Host workshops open to all students on topics like combinatorics, number theory, and advanced calculus problem-solving.

  • March 2026
    Integration Bee Training

    Focus practice sessions on integration techniques, speed, and accuracy in preparation for the internal competition.

  • April 2026
    Integration Bee Competition

    Host the fast-paced, bracket-style Integration Bee in collaboration with Dr. Schmidt. Open to all LU students.

📚 Resources

The Math Club provides several resources to support the Competition Team:

  • Problem Archives: Access to past problems from the VTRMC, Putnam, and other competitions.
  • Faculty Coaching: Direct guidance from faculty on advanced topics and competition strategies.
  • Strategy Workshops: Sessions focused not just on solving problems, but on how to approach competitions strategically.

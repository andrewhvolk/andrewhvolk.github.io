🎯 Our Mission

The Competition Team is for students who enjoy collaborative problem-solving and want to represent Liberty University at regional and national events. Participation sharpens analytical skills, builds teamwork, and provides a challenging outlet for your mathematical talent.

🚀 How to Get Involved

The Competition Team is open to all undergraduate students, regardless of major. Our activities are designed to be a fun way to engage with math. An interest meeting will be held at the beginning of the Fall semester. Come ready to solve some problems!

🗓️ 2025-2026 Featured Competitions

This year, we are focusing on three flagship events, each offering a unique challenge.

🤔 Fall 2025: The Estimathon

The Estimathon is a team-based contest that combines trivia, critical thinking, and mathematical estimation. Your team will have 30 minutes to tackle 13 challenging estimation problems, from "How many US gas stations have a convenience store?" to "What is the total length of all New York City subway lines?" It's a battle of wits and reasoning, no calculators allowed!

  • Focus: Real-world estimation, teamwork, creative problem-solving.
  • Get Ready: We'll host practice sessions to sharpen your estimation skills.

🧠 Fall 2025: The Putnam Competition

The William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition is the most prestigious undergraduate math competition in North America. It is a challenging six-hour exam known for its creative and difficult problems in advanced calculus, number theory, and abstract algebra. It's a fantastic way to test your proof-writing and problem-solving abilities at the highest level.

  • Focus: Rigorous proof-writing, advanced mathematical concepts.
  • Get Ready: "Putnam Challenge Mondays" will be held on the first Monday of each month to work through past problems and strategies.

⚡ Spring 2026: The Integration Bee

A fast-paced, bracket-style tournament to find Liberty University's best integrator! Contestants go head-to-head to solve calculus integration problems on a whiteboard in a race against the clock. This lively event, hosted in collaboration with Dr. Schmidt, is a favorite for both participants and spectators.

  • Focus: Speed and accuracy in calculus, single-elimination tournament excitement.
  • Get Ready: We will host integration-focused workshops in the spring to prepare.

📚 Resources

The Math Club provides several resources to support the Competition Team:

  • Problem Archives: Access to past problems from the VTRMC, Putnam, and other competitions.
  • Faculty Coaching: Direct guidance from faculty on advanced topics and competition strategies.
  • Strategy Workshops: Sessions focused not just on solving problems, but on how to approach competitions strategically.

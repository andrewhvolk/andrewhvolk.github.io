🔬 Math Club Research Team Plan

Structure, Goals, and Timeline for 2025-2026

Faculty Advisor: Mr. Andrew Volk

🎯 Mission & Core Goals

The Research Team track is designed to provide students with hands-on research experience under faculty mentorship. It is ideal for students preparing for graduate school or careers that require analytical and problem-solving skills.

Our primary goals for the 2025-2026 academic year are:
  • To produce one scholarly product per semester, such as a research paper or poster.
  • To provide opportunities for students to submit and present their work at a regional undergraduate research conference.
  • To develop practical skills in mathematical software (like Overleaf, Sage, etc.), literature review, and formal presentation.
  • To foster direct faculty mentorship relationships that guide students through the research process.

🏗️ Team Structure & Process

Project Formation

At the beginning of each semester, the faculty advisor will propose a few potential research problems. Students will form small teams (2-4 members) based on their interest in these problems.

How to Join

An interest meeting will be held in the first two weeks of the Fall semester. All members with a passion for problem-solving are welcome. A background in foundational math courses is helpful but not required.

🗓️ Proposed Semester Timeline

This timeline provides a framework for achieving our goal of completing one project and presenting at a conference each semester.

Fall 2025 Semester

Primary Target: SUMS Conference at JMU (Oct/Nov 2025) or NURVa Conference (Nov 1, 2025).
  • September 2025
    Team Formation & Problem Selection

    Form research teams, select a research problem, and begin initial literature review.

  • October 2025
    Active Research & Abstract Submission

    Dive into the problem-solving phase. Draft and submit abstracts to target conferences like SUMS or NURVa.

  • November 2025
    Finalize Results & Conference Presentation

    Complete the research phase, create a poster or slide deck, and present at the conference.

  • December 2025
    Project Write-Up

    Begin drafting a formal paper summarizing the research question, methodology, and findings.

Spring 2026 Semester

Primary Target: Rose-Hulman Conference (April 2026) or MORE Conference (Spring 2026).
  • January 2026
    Project Refinement or Selection

    Either continue and deepen the Fall project or select a new problem to tackle.

  • February 2026
    Intensive Research & Paper Writing

    Focus on advancing the research and making significant progress on the formal paper. Submit abstracts to spring conferences.

  • March 2026
    Prepare for Presentation

    Finalize the research paper and develop the accompanying presentation materials for the upcoming conference.

  • April 2026
    Conference Presentation & Final Submission

    Travel to and present at the target conference. Finalize the scholarly paper for the semester.

📚 Resources

The Math Club provides several resources to support the Research Team:

  • Conference Planning Resource: We maintain a comprehensive list of undergraduate-friendly conferences to help teams identify suitable venues for presentation.
  • Faculty Mentorship: Direct access to the faculty advisor for guidance, problem-solving, and networking.
  • Software Workshops: Opportunities to learn relevant software for research and typesetting, such as Overleaf (for LaTeX), Sage, or Jupyter Notebooks, through club-wide workshops.

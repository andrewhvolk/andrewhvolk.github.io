📝 GRE Study Group Series

A peer-led workshop series supporting students preparing for the GRE General Test, with emphasis on the Quantitative Reasoning section and mathematics relevant to graduate/professional school applications.

Overview

These sessions provide structured group practice, strategy-sharing, and encouragement for all stages of GRE prep. Each meeting spotlights key topics, with sample problems, review, and time for questions.

  • Focus: Quantitative Reasoning (algebra, data analysis, geometry, arithmetic, word problems).
  • Format: Brief review, collaborative problem sets, timed practice, and strategic discussion. Emphasis on growth and encouragement.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need to be taking the GRE soon to attend?
    No. Anyone interested in math practice or graduate study is welcome, regardless of test plans.
  • Are study materials provided?
    Yes. Sample problems, study guides, and solution walkthroughs are available for each session.
  • What if I need help with another section of the GRE?
    While focus is on Quantitative Reasoning, we will help direct you to verbal and analytical writing resources.

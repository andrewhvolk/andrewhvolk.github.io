🔢 Math Challenge Workshop Series

A structured set of six problem-solving workshops held across the academic year. The first four sessions prepare students for the Putnam Mathematical Competition (Fall). The final two are devoted to integration techniques and fast-paced calculus problems in anticipation of the Integration Bee (Spring).

Overview

Math Challenge Workshops are open to all interested students, regardless of competition intent. They offer intensive, collaborative, and supportive settings to develop creative mathematical thinking and advanced problem-solving skills.

  • Fall Workshops (4 sessions): Emphasize Putnam-style problems from algebra, combinatorics, calculus, geometry, and number theory.
  • Spring Workshops (2 sessions): Focus on advanced integration strategies, techniques, and competition-style speed rounds for the Integration Bee.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I have to compete in Putnam or the Integration Bee to attend?
    No. All are welcome, regardless of competition plans. Workshops benefit anyone interested in challenging math.
  • What level of math background is expected?
    Students who have completed Calculus I and have interest in problem-solving will benefit most. All levels are encouraged to participate.
  • Will solutions be provided?
    Yes. Each session includes instructor-led discussion, collaborative problem-solving, and full written solutions to all problems.

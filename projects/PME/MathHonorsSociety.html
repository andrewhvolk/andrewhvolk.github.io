🏛️ Math Honor Societies

Recognizing outstanding mathematical achievement at Liberty University.

✨ A Legacy of Excellence & A New Beginning

The Math Club is proud to facilitate the activities of the national mathematics honor societies on campus. These organizations provide a way to recognize students who have demonstrated a high level of scholarship and a deep interest in the mathematical sciences.

Kappa Mu Epsilon (KME)

Since 1989, Liberty University has been home to the Virginia Iota chapter of Kappa Mu Epsilon. For over three decades, KME has been our institution's primary mathematics honor society, inducting students who have shown exceptional ability and interest in the field. It has a rich history of fostering a community of scholars among our students and faculty.

Pi Mu Epsilon (PME)

Beginning in Fall 2025, we are thrilled to launch a new chapter of Pi Mu Epsilon, the National Mathematics Honor Society of the United States. PME is dedicated to the promotion of scholarly activity in mathematics among students. Establishing this chapter will open up new opportunities for national recognition, scholarships, and conference participation for our students.

📜 Membership & Induction

Membership in both KME and PME is by invitation to students who meet the required academic standards. While specific requirements may vary slightly, invitations are generally extended based on:

  • Completion of a minimum number of mathematics courses (such as Calculus I & II).
  • A high GPA in all mathematics courses.
  • A high overall GPA across all subjects.

The official induction ceremony for new members of Pi Mu Epsilon will be held in the Fall semester, with details announced at the beginning of the academic year.

💼 Career Prep Workshop Series

Recurring workshops dedicated to preparing students for internships, graduate school, and professional careers in mathematics and related fields. Each session offers actionable advice, resources, and peer/faculty support.

Overview

Career Prep workshops guide students through every step of academic and career development. Sessions feature presentations, interactive activities, and individualized support for application materials and career planning.

  • Audience: All math majors and minors, as well as anyone interested in mathematics-related careers or graduate school.
  • Topics: Résumés, CVs, cover letters, interview skills, networking, internships, graduate school admissions, and more.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need to attend every session?
    No. Attend as many as you wish. Each workshop is self-contained.
  • Can I get feedback on my materials?
    Yes. Faculty and peer review is available during every session.
  • Will there be guest speakers?
    Maybe. Sessions may include alumni, employers, or graduate students sharing insights and advice.

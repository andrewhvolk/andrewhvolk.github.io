💻 Learn & Play: Tech Workshop Series

A sequence of hands-on workshops throughout the academic year designed to introduce and practice essential mathematical software tools for coursework, research, and professional development.

Overview

Learn & Play workshops provide students with practical, guided experience using computational and communication tools common in academic mathematics and applied fields. Each session features live demos, interactive activities, and time to explore features at your own pace.

  • All levels welcome: No prior experience is assumed. Bring a laptop if possible; loaners are available.
  • Tool-focused sessions: Each meeting spotlights one technology, progressing from document preparation to symbolic computation, visualization, and data analysis.

**Prepared by:** Mr. Andrew Volk, Faculty Advisor

### I. Current Leadership Structure

**Officer Position Descriptions**

**🗣️ President:** The President acts as the primary spokesperson for the club and provides oversight for all official club activities. Additionally, the President is responsible for acting as a liaison among club members, organizing club activities, and overseeing the schedule.

**🎯 Vice President:** The Vice President assists with the facilitation of club activities and provides oversight in absence of the President. The Vice President should aid in the planning and preparation of club events.

**📋 Secretary:** The Secretary is responsible for keeping club records, including club attendance. The Secretary should assist with projects as needed and ensure that proper information is organized and distributed.

**💰 Treasurer:** The Treasurer receives and handles club money and monitors the budget. The Treasurer should keep a record of all transactions and ensure that established guidelines are met.

**🧠 Leadership Advisory Council:** The Council consists of active members who contribute ideas and support planning without necessarily holding formal officer roles. Council members serve as an extension of the leadership team and may take on projects, subcommittees, or represent student feedback.

### II. Club Structure & Engagement Tracks

**1. 🧠 Leadership Team**

* Includes all elected officers 🗣️🎯📋💰 and any volunteers serving in advisory roles 🧠
* Weekly 1:1 meetings between President 🗣️ and Faculty Advisor (in-person, phone, or Teams)
* Monthly all-officer 🗣️🎯📋💰 meetings (hybrid format)
* Advisory Council🧠: open to active members who wish to contribute ideas or serve on planning subcommittees

**2. 📚 Research Team**

* Objective: Produce one scholarly “product” (poster, paper, or presentation) per semester
* Teams will be mentored by a mathematics faculty member
* Opportunities for conference submission (e.g., JMU SUMS, PME regional meetings)
* Ideal for students considering graduate school or careers in research

**3. 🏆 Competition Team**

* Goal: Prepare for and participate in at least one mathematics competition each semester
* Includes problem-solving sessions and strategy workshops
* Open to all majors and levels of experience
* Example events: Putnam Exam, Integration Bee

**4. 🤝 Interdisciplinary Team**

* Acts as a consulting group for other LU departments
* Matches math majors with faculty-led research needing quantitative analysis
* Promotes mathematical literacy and application across disciplines

### IV. PME Chapter Notes

* Application to Pi Mu Epsilon (PME) national office is in progress
* Aim to hold first PME induction in Fall 2025
* All PME events coordinated by Math Club; no parallel governance structure
* Special considerations being discussed with PME regarding online student eligibility
* **PME Structure Alignment:**

  * PME President = Highest-ranking PME-inducted Math Club officer
  * All PME events and responsibilities will be coordinated through the Math Club
  * No separate PME leadership team; positions mirror Math Club roles where applicable

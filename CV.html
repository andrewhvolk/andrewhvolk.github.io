About

Andrew H. Volk is Assistant Professor of Mathematics and Physics at Liberty University, where he teaches quantitative reasoning, technical mathematics, and physics laboratories, and advises the Math Club, Pi Mu Epsilon, and Kappa Mu Epsilon. Before joining Liberty in 2020, he taught and chaired mathematics in Virginia public schools and received the VCTM William C. Lowry Math Educator of the Year Award. His research examines artificial intelligence in higher education, behavioral nudges, and participation in STEM. He completed his Ph.D. in Engineering at Mississippi State University in August 2026 and also holds graduate degrees in mathematics, physics teaching, and instructional design.

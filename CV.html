Professional Profile

Professional Summary

Assistant Professor of Mathematics and Physics with experience spanning university teaching, secondary mathematics leadership, curriculum design, and educational technology. His work centers on helping students grow through clear instruction, mastery-oriented assessment, and mathematically humane teaching practices.

Current scholarship and presentations focus on mathematics education, reflective practice, digital tools for learning, and interdisciplinary inquiry that connects mathematics to broader human questions.

Teaching Profile

Teaching Philosophy

Learning is about change and growth. Teaching therefore aims not merely at the transfer of information, but at cultivating durable intellectual development, reflective habits, and confidence in students as learners.

Project Rhythm

How the 2026-2027 plan is structured.

  1. September 2026: Outreach and partnership building.
    Introduce the team to departments such as Biology, Business, Psychology, and Engineering.
  2. October 2026: Interdisciplinary project pitch event.
    Campus partners pitch research problems and project teams form around interest and skills.
  3. November 2026: Project work and initial analysis.
    Teams clarify the problem, clean data, and begin analysis or modeling.
  4. Spring 2027: Deep work, final reporting, and showcase.
    Teams develop models, run analyses, prepare results, and share collaborative work.

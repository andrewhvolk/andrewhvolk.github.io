Ask About Roles

Role Guide

Officer responsibilities.

Use these expandable notes when preparing for officer work or considering a role.

President

Acts as the primary spokesperson, provides oversight for club activities, liaises with faculty and members, organizes meetings, and guides the club's overall direction.

Vice President

Assists the President, facilitates club activities, provides leadership coverage, supports event planning, and helps other officers execute their work.

Secretary

Maintains meeting minutes, attendance, and club records, and helps organize information for members.

Treasurer and Treasury Analyst

Manage finances, monitor budget activity, track transactions, analyze spending trends, and prepare financial summaries for meetings.

Competition Lead

Coordinates participation in Estimathon, MORE, SUMS, Putnam, and Integration Bee, manages registration, and supports competitors.

Research Team Lead

Leads research team meetings, allocates work, reviews research products, manages project timelines, and serves as the point of contact for supervising faculty.

Social Media and Creative Manager

Prepares announcements, digital communication, flyers, certificates, merchandise, and visual branding materials.

Event Volunteers

Help with attendance, room setup, food requests, workshop materials, and follow-up communication.

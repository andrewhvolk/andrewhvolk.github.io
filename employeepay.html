The math behind it

With a fixed wage w and a starting pay p that doubles every day:

Fixed job, any day: w  ·  total after n days: w · n (linear)
Doubling job, day n: p · 2n − 1  ·  total after n days: p · (2n − 1) (exponential)

Day 1 has no doublings yet, which is why day n uses the exponent n − 1. The total is one day's pay short of doubling once more: 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 = 15 = 16 − 1. So each day's doubling pay is larger than all the earlier days put together.

1. Sketching an angle with absolute value less than 360 degrees in standard position

  • Q1: Sketch the angle 150° in standard position.
  • Q2: Sketch the angle -85° in standard position.
  • Q3: Sketch the angle 210° in standard position.
Show answers

  • Q2: −85° turns clockwise and ends in Quadrant IV, 5° short of the negative y-axis.
  • Q3: 210° ends in Quadrant III, 30° past the negative x-axis.

4. Sketching an angle with absolute value greater than 360 degrees in standard position

  • Q1: Sketch the angle 420° in standard position.
  • Q2: Sketch the angle -400° in standard position.
  • Q3: Sketch the angle 13π/3 in standard position.
Show answers
  • Q1: 420° is one full turn plus 60°, so it ends in Quadrant I like 60°.
  • Q2: −400° is one full clockwise turn plus 40° more, so it ends in Quadrant IV like 320°.
  • Q3: 13π/3 is two full turns plus π/3, so it ends in Quadrant I like π/3.

5. Arc length and central angle measure

  • Q1: A circle has a radius of 4 feet. Find the exact arc length intercepted by a central angle of π/3 radians.
  • Q2: Find the distance a wheel of diameter 3 ft travels in one complete rotation.
  • Q3: A central angle of 120° intercepts an arc of 15 inches. Find the radius of the circle.
Show answers
  • Q1: s = rθ = 4(π/3) = 4π/3 ft ≈ 4.19 ft.
  • Q2: One rotation is the circumference: 3π ft ≈ 9.42 ft.
  • Q3: 120° = 2π/3, so r = s/θ = 15 ÷ (2π/3) = 45/(2π) ≈ 7.16 in.

7. Sine, cosine, and tangent ratios: Numbers for side lengths

  • Q1: Given a right triangle with legs 3 and 4, and hypotenuse 5, find sin(θ) where θ is opposite the side of length 3.
  • Q2: In a right triangle where the adjacent side to θ is 8 and the hypotenuse is 17, find cos(θ).
  • Q3: For a right triangle with legs 5 and 12, find tan(θ) for the angle adjacent to the side of length 12.
Show answers

  • Q2: cos θ = 8/17.
  • Q3: The angle adjacent to 12 is opposite 5, so tan θ = 5/12.

8. Sine, cosine, and tangent ratios: Variables for side lengths

  • Q1: A right triangle has legs x and y, and hypotenuse z. Write the ratio for sin(θ) if θ is opposite x.
  • Q2: In a right triangle with hypotenuse c and legs a and b, what is tan(θ) for the angle adjacent to a?
  • Q3: If a right triangle has an adjacent leg m and opposite leg n, express cos(θ) in terms of m and n.
Show answers
  • Q1: sin θ = x/z.
  • Q2: tan θ = b/a.
  • Q3: cos θ = m/√(m² + n²).

9. Using the Pythagorean Theorem to find a sine, cosine, or tangent ratio in a right triangle

  • Q1: If sin(θ) = 3/5 in a right triangle, find cos(θ).
  • Q2: If cos(θ) = 7/25 in a right triangle, find tan(θ).
  • Q3: Given tan(θ) = 8/15, use the Pythagorean theorem to find sin(θ).
Show answers
  • Q1: Opposite 3, hypotenuse 5, adjacent 4: cos θ = 4/5.
  • Q2: Adjacent 7, hypotenuse 25, opposite 24: tan θ = 24/7.
  • Q3: Opposite 8, adjacent 15, hypotenuse 17: sin θ = 8/17.

10. Using the Pythagorean Theorem to find several trigonometric ratios in a right triangle

  • Q1: A right triangle has legs 6 and 8. Find all six trigonometric ratios for the angle opposite the side of length 6.
  • Q2: If the hypotenuse is 13 and one leg is 5, find csc(θ) and sec(θ) for the angle adjacent to the leg of length 5.
  • Q3: Given sin(θ) = 9/41, find the exact values of the remaining five trigonometric functions.
Show answers

  • Q2: The other leg is 12. csc θ = 13/12 and sec θ = 13/5.
  • Q3: Adjacent side 40. cos θ = 40/41, tan θ = 9/40, csc θ = 41/9, sec θ = 41/40, cot θ = 40/9.

11. Using a trigonometric ratio to find a side length in a right triangle

  • Q1: In a right triangle with an angle of 30° and a hypotenuse of 20, find the length of the opposite leg.
  • Q2: Find the adjacent leg of a right triangle if the angle is 45° and the hypotenuse is 10.
  • Q3: If an angle is 60° and the opposite leg is 15√3, find the hypotenuse.
Show answers
  • Q1: 20 sin 30° = 10.
  • Q2: 10 cos 45° = 5√2 ≈ 7.07.
  • Q3: 15√3 ÷ sin 60° = 30.

12. Using trigonometry to find a length in a word problem with one right triangle

  • Q1: A ladder leans against a building making an angle of 70° with the ground. If the base of the ladder is 4 ft from the wall, how long is the ladder?
  • Q2: A kite is flying on 150 feet of string. If the angle of elevation is 40°, how high is the kite?
  • Q3: A tree casts a 20-foot shadow when the sun's angle of elevation is 55°. Find the height of the tree.
Show answers
  • Q1: cos 70° = 4/L, so L = 4 ÷ cos 70° ≈ 11.70 ft.
  • Q2: h = 150 sin 40° ≈ 96.42 ft.
  • Q3: h = 20 tan 55° ≈ 28.56 ft.

13. Using trigonometry to find lengths in a figure involving two right triangles

  • Q1: Two buildings are 50 ft apart. From the top of the shorter building, the angle of elevation to the taller is 30° and the angle of depression to its base is 45°. Find the height of the taller building.
  • Q2: A surveyor is 100 ft from a tower. The angle of elevation to the top of a given antenna on the tower is 60°, and to the bottom of the antenna is 45°. Find the antenna's length.
  • Q3: From a boat, the angle of elevation to the top of a cliff is 25°. After sailing 200 ft closer, the angle is 40°. How high is the cliff?
Show answers
  • Q1: The 45° depression makes the shorter building 50 ft tall. The rise above it is 50 tan 30° ≈ 28.87 ft, so the taller building is about 78.87 ft.
  • Q2: 100 tan 60° − 100 tan 45° ≈ 173.21 − 100 = 73.21 ft.
  • Q3: With h = d tan 25° = (d − 200) tan 40°, d ≈ 450.2 ft and h ≈ 209.9 ft.

22. Using a trigonometric ratio to find a side length in a right triangle

  • Q1: Solve for leg a in a right triangle where A = 35° and hypotenuse c = 14.
  • Q2: Solve for leg b in a right triangle where B = 50° and hypotenuse c = 22.
  • Q3: Solve for leg a in a right triangle where B = 25° and leg b = 10.
Show answers
  • Q1: a = 14 sin 35° ≈ 8.03.
  • Q2: b = 22 sin 50° ≈ 16.85.
  • Q3: a = b ÷ tan 25° = 10 ÷ tan 25° ≈ 21.45.

23. Using trigonometry to find a length in a word problem with one right triangle

  • Q1: A ship travels 80 miles on a bearing of 120°. How far east has it traveled?
  • Q2: A plane flies 150 miles on a bearing of 210°. How far south is it from the starting point?
  • Q3: A hiker walks 5 miles east and 12 miles north. What is the direct distance from the start?
Show answers
  • Q1: Bearing 120° is 30° south of east. East: 80 sin 120° ≈ 69.28 mi.
  • Q2: Bearing 210° is 30° west of south. South: 150 cos 30° ≈ 129.90 mi.
  • Q3: √(5² + 12²) = 13 mi.

24. Using trigonometric functions and the formula d=rt in a real-world situation

  • Q1: An airplane flies at 400 mph on a bearing of S30°W for 2 hours. How far south has it traveled?
  • Q2: A submarine descends at an angle of 5° at 15 mph. How deep is it after 30 minutes?
  • Q3: A plane climbs at 12° at 300 mph. How long to reach an altitude of 3 miles?
Show answers
  • Q1: Distance 800 mi. South: 800 cos 30° ≈ 692.82 mi.
  • Q2: Distance 7.5 mi. Depth: 7.5 sin 5° ≈ 0.654 mi ≈ 3,451 ft.
  • Q3: Climb rate: 300 sin 12° ≈ 62.37 mph, so 3 ÷ 62.37 ≈ 0.048 h ≈ 2.9 minutes.

25. Using a trigonometric ratio to find an angle measure in a right triangle

  • Q1: In a right triangle, leg a = 15 and leg b = 20. Find angle A.

27. Solving a right triangle

  • Q2: Solve the right triangle where a = 18 and b = 24.

28. Using trigonometry to find a length in a word problem with two right triangles

  • Q1: From a window 40 ft above ground, the angle of elevation to the top of a neighboring building is 20° and the angle of depression to the base is 35°. Find the building's height.
  • Q2: A person is standing between two trees. The angle of elevation to the top of the 30 ft tree is 45° and to the top of the 40 ft tree is 60°. How far apart are the trees?
  • Q3: From the top of a 150 ft lighthouse, the angles of depression to two boats in a line are 25° and 40°. Find the distance between the boats.
Show answers
  • Q1: Distance to the building: 40 ÷ tan 35° ≈ 57.13 ft. Height: 40 + 57.13 tan 20° ≈ 60.79 ft.
  • Q2: The person is 30 ft from the 30 ft tree and 40 ÷ tan 60° ≈ 23.09 ft from the 40 ft tree, so the trees are about 53.09 ft apart (measuring from ground level).
  • Q3: 150 ÷ tan 25° − 150 ÷ tan 40° ≈ 321.68 − 178.76 ≈ 142.91 ft.

33. Finding the components of a vector given its graph

  • Q1: Given a vector drawn from (2, 1) to (5, 6) on a grid, determine its x and y components.
  • Q2: Read the graph of vector r pointing left 4 units and up 2 units. Write its component form.
  • Q3: From a graph showing u and v added head-to-tail, determine the component form of the resultant r.
Show answers
  • Q1: ⟨3, 5⟩.
  • Q2: ⟨−4, 2⟩.
  • Q3: The resultant is r = u + v: add the x-components of u and v, then the y-components.

The Church and Christianity in 2025

Embracing Technology

Leverage technology for outreach and community building. The digital world is a mission field.

Standing for Social Justice

Defend the oppressed, speak for the voiceless, and work towards a world where all people are treated with dignity and respect.

Engaging in Interfaith Dialogue

Listen to and understand those who hold different beliefs. Seek common ground and build bridges of understanding.

Practicing Environmental Stewardship

Care for the earth and its resources. Reduce our carbon footprint, conserve energy, and advocate for sustainable practices.

Awareness of -isms

Be aware of the dangers of tribalism, denominationalism, and nationalism. Strive to see beyond our own groups and recognize our shared humanity.

Remaining Relevant

Adapt to new challenges and opportunities, while remaining grounded in the unchanging truth of the Gospel.

Living out our faith in bold and transformative ways.

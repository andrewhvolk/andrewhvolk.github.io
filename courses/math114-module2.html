MATH 114 · Module 2 of 14

Problem Solving & Units

Most real-world math comes with units attached. This module shows you how to track units, convert between them, and use them to guide your problem solving.

  • Chapter 2
  • Unit analysis

Learn the Concepts

1 · Working with units

A number alone rarely tells the whole story — 5 means something very different as 5 dollars, 5 miles, or 5 hours. The unit is part of the answer.

  • "Per" means division: miles per hour is miles ÷ hours.
  • "Of" often means multiplication: 0.25 of 80 is 0.25 × 80.
  • Square / cubic units come from multiplying lengths: ft × ft = ft² (area), ft × ft × ft = ft³ (volume).

Learn the Concepts

2 · Unit (dimensional) analysis

Treat units like numbers you can multiply and cancel. A conversion factor is a fraction equal to 1, such as (5280 ft) / (1 mi). Multiplying by it doesn't change the amount — only the units it's written in. Line up the factors so the units you don't want cancel and the units you do want remain.

A dependable four-step strategy for any problem:

  1. Understand — what is given, and what are you asked to find (in what units)?
  2. Plan — choose conversion factors or a formula that connects them.
  3. Solve — carry out the arithmetic, keeping units attached.
  4. Check — do the units and the size of the answer make sense?

Worked Examples

See it done, step by step

Try each first, then reveal the solution.

Example 1 — Convert 60 miles per hour to feet per second

Chain conversion factors so "miles" and "hours" cancel, leaving feet over seconds.

60 mi/hr × (5280 ft / 1 mi) × (1 hr / 3600 s)

= (60 × 5280 ÷ 3600) ft/s = 88 ft/s.

Example 2 — A car travels 360 miles on 12 gallons. What is its gas mileage?

Gas mileage is measured in miles per gallon, so divide miles by gallons.

360 mi ÷ 12 gal = 30 miles per gallon.

Example 3 — How many square feet of carpet for a 12 ft by 10 ft room?

Area of a rectangle is length × width, and ft × ft = ft².

12 ft × 10 ft = 120 square feet.

Check Yourself

Quick self-check

Answer, then reveal.

1. Convert 3 feet to inches.

3 ft × (12 in / 1 ft) = 36 inches.

2. Apples cost $1.50 per pound. What do 4 pounds cost?

4 lb × ($1.50 / 1 lb) = $6.00.

3. A trip of 150 miles at 50 miles per hour takes how long?

150 mi ÷ (50 mi/hr) = 3 hours.

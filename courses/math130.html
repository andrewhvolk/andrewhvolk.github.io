MATH 130: Advanced Technical Mathematics

Learn each unit, practice, and walk into your in-class quizzes and tests ready. Canvas is the master schedule; ALEKS homework is online, while quizzes and tests are on paper in class unless announced otherwise.

  • Fall 2026 course hub

How MATH 130 works

  • Learn and practice here — use the unit hubs for slides, videos, formulas, and review tools.
  • Complete ALEKS through Canvas — Canvas controls assignment availability, dates, and times.
  • Take quizzes and tests in class, on paper — Canvas may show a matching gradebook date, but that does not make the assessment an online submission.
  • Treat Canvas as the source of truth — this site mirrors the schedule and supports preparation; Canvas or an in-class announcement can adjust assessment coverage.

Getting ready for a quiz or test

  1. Open the unit you're working on and learn the topic with its slides and videos.
  2. Do the matching homework in Canvas.
  3. Practice with the unit's review tools and mock quizzes until the steps feel automatic.
  4. Check the date and sections for your next quiz or test, then take it in class on paper.

Course Units & Study Materials

Choose a unit and open its review hub.

The cards below organize resources for Test 1 through the Final Exam in one place.

Unit 1 / Test 1

Foundations and algebra fluency

Topic focus: algebra foundations, linear equations and lines, functions and coordinate geometry, then quadratic applications.

Recommended start: follow Unit 1A–1D in order, complete the targeted practice and checkpoint after each, then use Unit 1E for mixed Test 1 retrieval.

Main resources: five offline-capable HTML decks, staged practice pools, quiz-window checkpoints, topic reference, formulas, and progress history.

View Unit 1 Sequence
Unit 2 / Test 2

Interest, logarithms, and geometry

Topic focus: interest, logarithms, polygon geometry, circle geometry, and applications.

Recommended start: open the Test 2 review tabs and follow the guided checklist flow.

Main resources: review tabs, formula work, practice sets, and quiz-style checks.

View Unit 2 Resources
Unit 3 / Test 3

Trigonometry and vectors

Topic focus: Sections 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.7, 7.1, and 8.4 with Test 3 review tools.

Recommended start: use the section overview first, then move into formulas and practice.

Main resources: guided summaries, checklist items, formulas, and mock quiz tools.

View Unit 3 Resources
Final Exam Review

Cumulative final practice

Topic focus: cumulative algebra, geometry, trigonometry, applications, and vectors from the current practice blueprint.

Recommended start: use the guided review to identify priorities, then work the interactive practice lab.

Main resources: guided final-review lecture, formula reminders, generated practice, hints, and worked solutions.

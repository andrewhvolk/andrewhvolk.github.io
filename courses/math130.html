How MATH 130 works
  1. Learn the current topic with its lesson materials.
  2. Complete the matching ALEKS work through Canvas.
  3. Use targeted practice, then take the checkpoint.
  4. Take quizzes and tests on paper in class unless announced otherwise.

Canvas is the source of truth for dates and coverage changes.

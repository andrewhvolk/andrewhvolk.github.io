ENGI 220: Engineering Economy

A long-form course shell for financial decision tools, problem-solving workflows, and reference materials.

Course overview

ENGI 220 introduces the economic reasoning engineers use to compare alternatives, analyze cash flow, and justify decisions with clear quantitative evidence.

Instructional focus

  1. Model cash-flow situations and identify economic decision criteria.
  2. Use present worth, future worth, and annual worth methods to compare alternatives.
  3. Interpret rates of return, replacement timing, inflation, and cost behavior.
  4. Document assumptions carefully so recommendations remain transparent.
  5. Review major formulas and calculator workflows before quizzes and exams.

Suggested workflow

  1. Start each week by identifying the vocabulary, formulas, and examples emphasized in class.
  2. Work through guided examples before independent homework so methods stay organized.
  3. Show steps clearly, label units when relevant, and keep track of questions for class or office hours.
  4. Use quizzes, review sheets, and prior assignments to identify patterns in mistakes.
  5. Revisit this page as a stable landing point for archived resources and future additions.

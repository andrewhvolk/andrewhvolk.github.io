ENGI 220: Engineering Economy

Applying economic analysis to engineering design and decision making.

Course Overview

Engineering Economy introduces the analytic tools needed to evaluate design alternatives, capital investments, and strategic decisions. Students learn to quantify time value of money, compare mutually exclusive options, and communicate recommendations using clear financial justification.

Key Topics

  • Simple and compound interest, effective rates, and cash-flow diagrams
  • Present, future, and annual worth analysis for project evaluation
  • Rate of return methods and incremental analysis
  • Break-even, payback, and benefit-cost comparisons
  • Inflation, taxes, and replacement analysis considerations

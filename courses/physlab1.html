PHYS 201L / 231L: Physics Lab 1

A long-form lab home for first-semester physics experiments, preparation, and course guidance.

Course overview

Physics Lab 1 supports mechanics-centered experiments that ask students to measure physical quantities carefully, compare theory with data, and document uncertainty responsibly.

Instructional focus

  1. Review each experiment before lab so the setup and target quantities are familiar.
  2. Prepare units, formulas, and diagram conventions before collecting data.
  3. Record measurements clearly enough that another student could follow your work.
  4. Use graphs, slopes, and uncertainty language when interpreting results.
  5. Return to manuals and notes when preparing reports or exam review.

Suggested workflow

  1. Start each week by identifying the vocabulary, formulas, and examples emphasized in class.
  2. Work through guided examples before independent homework so methods stay organized.
  3. Show steps clearly, label units when relevant, and keep track of questions for class or office hours.
  4. Use quizzes, review sheets, and prior assignments to identify patterns in mistakes.
  5. Revisit this page as a stable landing point for archived resources and future additions.

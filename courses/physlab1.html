Course Overview

What this lab page is for.

Course identity

PHYS 201L and PHYS 231L share this Physics Lab 1 resource page. If Canvas lists either code, this is the correct lab hub.

Instructional focus

Physics Lab 1 emphasizes mechanics-centered measurements, graphing, uncertainty, and clear comparison between physical models and collected data.

Instructor contact

For section-specific questions, use Canvas messages or email ahvolk@liberty.edu.

Before you come to lab

  1. Check Canvas for the assigned experiment, due date, and any instructor notes for your section.
  2. Read the relevant lab manual pages before class and identify the objective, apparatus, and major procedure steps.
  3. Write down expected quantities, units, formulas, and graph relationships before collecting data.
  4. Bring the required notebook, data sheet, calculator, and any files or printouts requested in Canvas.
  5. Ask before changing a setup, using unfamiliar equipment, or continuing when a measurement seems unsafe.

Safety

Equipment setup

Follow the manual and instructor directions exactly before releasing carts, hanging masses, adjusting sensors, or changing any setup.

Reports & Data Analysis

Make your lab work traceable.

Show the measurement trail

Include raw data, units, sample calculations, and graph labels so another reader can follow how each result was produced.

Connect results to the model

Compare slopes, fitted values, or calculated quantities with the expected physical relationship. Explain differences with evidence from the experiment.

Use uncertainty language

State major sources of uncertainty, distinguish random variation from systematic effects, and avoid unsupported claims about accuracy.

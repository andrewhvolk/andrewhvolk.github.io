Course Overview

What this lab page is for.

Course identity

PHYS 202L and PHYS 232L share this Physics Lab 2 resource page. If Canvas lists either code, this is the correct lab hub.

Instructional focus

Physics Lab 2 emphasizes careful setup, measurement, and interpretation in electricity, magnetism, and optical systems.

Instructor contact

For section-specific questions, use Canvas messages or email ahvolk@liberty.edu.

Before you come to lab

  1. Check Canvas for the assigned experiment, due date, and any instructor notes for your section.
  2. Read the relevant lab manual pages before class and identify the objective, apparatus, and major procedure steps.
  3. Review setup diagrams, polarity, alignment, calibration, formulas, and safety notes before touching equipment.
  4. Bring the required notebook, data sheet, calculator, and any files or printouts requested in Canvas.
  5. Ask before changing a circuit, optical path, sensor configuration, or unfamiliar equipment setup.

Equipment setup

Reports & Data Analysis

Make your lab work traceable.

Document the setup

Include circuit diagrams, optical arrangements, instrument settings, units, and any calibration steps needed to understand the data.

Connect observations to theory

Use graphs, slopes, fitted values, or calculated quantities to connect measurements with the expected physical relationship.

Explain limitations

Discuss alignment, calibration, resistance, field effects, reading uncertainty, or other sources that shape the reliability of your result.

PHYS 202L / 232L: Physics Lab 2

A long-form lab home for second-semester experiments in electricity, magnetism, and optics.

Course overview

Physics Lab 2 organizes later-semester investigations around careful setup, measurement, and interpretation in electricity, magnetism, and optical systems.

Instructional focus

  1. Preview the experiment and identify the instruments used that day.
  2. Pay attention to setup diagrams, polarity, alignment, and calibration steps.
  3. Record data in tables as you work so later analysis remains traceable.
  4. Use the manual discussion prompts to connect observations with theory.
  5. Keep a running reference list of formulas, units, and common conversions.

Suggested workflow

  1. Start each week by identifying the vocabulary, formulas, and examples emphasized in class.
  2. Work through guided examples before independent homework so methods stay organized.
  3. Show steps clearly, label units when relevant, and keep track of questions for class or office hours.
  4. Use quizzes, review sheets, and prior assignments to identify patterns in mistakes.
  5. Revisit this page as a stable landing point for archived resources and future additions.

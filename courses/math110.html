MATH 110: Intermediate Algebra

A long-form course shell for algebra progression, unit review, and ongoing support resources.

Course overview

MATH 110 extends foundational algebra into polynomial, rational, exponential, and systems work while reinforcing mathematical communication and multi-step problem solving.

Instructional focus

  1. Review foundational equations and algebraic structure.
  2. Practice factoring, polynomial operations, and rational expressions.
  3. Analyze exponential and logarithmic relationships.
  4. Solve systems and applied problems with organized algebra steps.
  5. Use review cycles to connect techniques across units.

Suggested workflow

  1. Start each week by identifying the vocabulary, formulas, and examples emphasized in class.
  2. Work through guided examples before independent homework so methods stay organized.
  3. Show steps clearly, label units when relevant, and keep track of questions for class or office hours.
  4. Use quizzes, review sheets, and prior assignments to identify patterns in mistakes.
  5. Revisit this page as a stable landing point for archived resources and future additions.

How to use this page

Start with the course home for your lab enrollment. Each home page collects the relevant manuals, experiment sequence, and course-specific notes in one place.

  1. Identify the course number listed in Canvas or on your syllabus.
  2. Open the matching course home below.
  3. Use that course page as your starting point for manuals, experiment preparation, and lab-day reference material.

Before you come to lab

  1. Read the assigned manual and note the objective, materials, and major procedure steps.
  2. Bring a notebook or data sheet so you can record measurements clearly during the experiment.
  3. Review any formulas or safety notes before class so that lab time can focus on observation and analysis.

Before you come to lab

  1. Read the assigned manual and note the objective, materials, and major procedure steps.
  2. Bring a notebook or data sheet so you can record measurements clearly during the experiment.
  3. Review any formulas or safety notes before class so lab time can focus on observation and analysis.

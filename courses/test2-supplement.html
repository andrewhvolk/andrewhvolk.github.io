Loans & Lump Sums

Loan payment (installment loan)

PMT = P × (APR/n)1 − (1 + APR/n)−nY

P = amount borrowed. Note the negative exponent. This is different from the savings-plan formula.

Deposit needed now (present value)

P = A(1 + APR/n)nY

Daily compounding: n = 365. Monthly: n = 12. Quarterly: n = 4.

Years to reach a target balance

Y = log(A/P)n × log(1 + APR/n)

Monthly interest on a balance

Monthly interest = balance × APR12

Simple vs. compound interest

  • Simple: interest paid only on the original principal.
  • Compound: interest paid on principal plus earlier interest.

Stocks

P/E ratio = share priceearnings per share  ⇒  EPS = share priceP/E
  • P/E above its historical range → stock seems overpriced.
  • P/E below its historical range → stock seems underpriced.

Budgeting

Convert everything to the same time period

  • Weekly → monthly: × 4 (when told 1 month = 4 weeks)
  • Annual → monthly: ÷ 12   Semiannual → monthly: ÷ 6   Quarterly → monthly: ÷ 3
  • Daily → annual: × 365   Monthly → annual: × 12
Net monthly cash flow = monthly income − monthly expenses

Negative cash flow means spending more than you earn.

Percent of budget = category spendingincome × 100%

Compare that percent to the chart's national average for the person's age group.

Cost of a class vs. working = tuition + wages you give up

Statistics Concepts

  • Qualitative: categories or labels (professions, colors). Quantitative: numbers you can count or measure (population, height).
  • Sampling: simple random means every member has an equal chance (names drawn from a hat). Systematic means every k-th member. Stratified means you split into groups and sample from each. Convenience means whoever is easy to reach.
  • Correlation: points rising left to right are positive, and falling is negative. Points close to a line mean a strong correlation, and loosely scattered points mean a weak one. No pattern means no correlation.
  • Bar graphs: describe each group's trend separately (steady increase, peak then decrease, and so on).

Algebra Review

  • Exponent rules: am × an = am+n and a1/2 = √a
  • Solving equations: undo operations in reverse order (add or subtract first, then multiply or divide). Check by plugging your answer back in.

Calculator Tips

  • Write percents as decimals (4% → 0.04).
  • Put whole exponents in parentheses: ^(12*19)
  • Don't round until the final answer.

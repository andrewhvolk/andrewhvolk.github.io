MATH 105: Algebra for Non-STEM Majors

A practical course shell for foundational algebra, course planning, and student support resources.

Course overview

MATH 105 introduces core algebra ideas through everyday problem solving, numerical reasoning, and interpretation of formulas, graphs, and quantitative statements.

Students should use this page as a stable reference point for the course structure, recurring study habits, and the kinds of resources that support each unit.

Course roadmap

  1. Review arithmetic fluency, signed numbers, and order of operations.
  2. Practice solving equations and inequalities step by step.
  3. Interpret formulas, tables, and graphs in applied settings.
  4. Use algebra to model practical situations and justify conclusions.
  5. Prepare for cumulative review with mixed practice and reflection.

Suggested weekly workflow

  1. Read the module overview and identify the target skills for the week.
  2. Complete guided notes or instructor examples before starting homework.
  3. Work assigned practice carefully, showing algebra steps in order.
  4. Check difficult problems with office hours, class notes, or approved videos.
  5. Summarize common mistakes before quizzes and unit reviews.

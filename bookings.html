Open Microsoft Bookings

How to book a meeting

  1. Open the Microsoft Bookings page using the button above.
  2. Review the available meeting options and choose the appointment type that best matches your needs.
  3. Select an open date and time from the calendar.
  4. Enter your name, email address, and any requested details.
  5. Add a short note about what you would like to discuss so Professor Volk can prepare.
  6. Submit the booking and look for the confirmation email from Microsoft Bookings.

Helpful tips

  • Use your Liberty email when appropriate so your appointment is easier to identify.
  • Include your course number or section if your question is class-related.
  • Check the confirmation email for the meeting time, location, or online meeting link.
  • If you need to change plans, use the cancellation or rescheduling options in the confirmation message when available.

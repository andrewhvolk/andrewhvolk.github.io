record can be checked rather than taken on trust.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE COMPONENT WITH NO SURVIVING PROJECT FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

No saved project survives for "Frame and Slider Assembly 2.0", the second
iteration of the adjustable frame. A print-monitor screenshot taken during that
job records 201 layers, 68.18 g and 2 h 12 m 47 s. The assembly measures
20.0 mm tall in its CAD. A 20 mm part needs 100 layers at 0.20 mm and 200 at
0.10 mm, so that print appears to have run at roughly 0.10 mm - finer than its
24 March predecessor.

This is an inference from two measurements, not a reading from a file. It is
recorded here because it matters to anyone reproducing the part, and flagged
because it is weaker evidence than everything above it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLEARANCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The four clearance values in the SOLIDWORKS model are TOTALS, not per-side
figures. Their own comment fields say so. A replicator who applies them per
side gets twice the intended slop.

    Frame tolerance   0.16 mm total   ("the total extra space on the top and
                                        bottom of the frames")
    Track tolerance   0.08 mm total   ("the total extra width of the track")
    Slider tolerance  0.16 mm total   ("the total extra width on the inside of
                                        the slider, so it wont grip the frame
                                        too much")
    Pin tolerance     0.16 mm total   ("the total subtracted width of the pin
                                        so it wont grip the sliders too much")

Per side that is 0.08 mm on the frame, slider and pin, and 0.04 mm on the
track. These are specific to the printer, material and settings above;
replication on another machine will need recalibration.
